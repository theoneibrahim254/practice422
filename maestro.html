Good to know

Questions before you get started?

Every project is different. These answers cover the basics; your enquiry can include anything else on your mind.

What kind of website projects do you take on?

Business, academic, ecommerce, portfolio, landing page, community, booking, redesign, and custom web projects are all welcome. Use the enquiry form to describe your needs.

How much will my website cost?

Cost depends on the pages, features, content, and overall scope. Once we understand your goals, we can discuss a suitable approach and estimate before work begins.

How long does a project take?

Timing varies with the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback are available. We will talk through a realistic schedule when we define the project.

Do I need to have everything ready first?

No. A rough idea is enough to start a conversation. We can identify the information, images, and decisions needed as the project takes shape.

Can you update or improve a website I already have?

Yes. Let us know what you would like to change, what is working well, and share the current website address in your message.
